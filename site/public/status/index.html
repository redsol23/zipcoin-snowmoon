Status

Is the network up? The postman and every bonded courier, checked once a minute.

Goes live at launch. zipcoin isn't on mainnet yet, so there's nothing to check. What each line will mean is below.

The postman

It approves deposits for private withdrawal and publishes the approved set's root once per epoch. Couriers need a fresh root to carry proofs.

Couriers

Anyone can run one by bonding ZC. They carry proofs to the chain so your wallet never has to, and a courier that breaks a signed promise loses part of its bond.

Couriers appear here once they bond, at launch.

How to read this

  • Answering

    The service replied to a health check within four seconds. A courier that doesn't answer can't take your job, and the wallet picks another.

  • Last root published

    How long ago the postman published an approved-deposit root, read from the chain. It should stay under one epoch; if it doesn't, new deposits wait, and anyone can still take theirs back with a ragequit.

  • Last job

    When a courier last sent anything, its own cover traffic included, to the minute. It says nothing about whose job it was.

  • Bond

    The ZC a courier has at stake. Leaving takes 14 days, so a broken promise can still be reported. Couriers that are leaving are marked and not checked.

Read from Ethereum and from each service's public health check, once a minute, by api.zipcoin.org.

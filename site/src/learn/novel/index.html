The novel

Each feature of the book's zipcoin, chapter by chapter, next to the contract that builds it.

What the novel imagined, and what's here

In Snowmoon, the zipcoin is more than a private way to pay. People knock at a door with a burn, show their reputation without their name, and pay to poll a whole society. Each of those is a contract here.

Features of the novel's zipcoin and how this project builds each one

Private payments ch. 6, 19Zip coins into a shared pool, and spend them with a zero-knowledge proof.0xbow Privacy Pools

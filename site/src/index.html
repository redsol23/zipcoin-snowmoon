Tax that keeps the market whole

Planned: the treasury's share of the sales tax goes into ZC's pool as liquidity above the price range the launch position covers, automatically and by fixed rules, so the market keeps depth past the point where the launch position ends. Nothing is added below that range: ETH that reaches the bands goes to the treasury, not into the pool. The positions and their fees belong to the project treasury. This isn't price support, and it doesn't pay holders.
